## Methodology review sources checked on 7 October 2026

- Amarilli and Paperman (2018): [publisher record and abstract](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.ICALP.2018.115) verify ICALP, volume 107, pp. 115:1–115:14, DOI and NP-hardness for fixed language (ab)*. General explicit-DFA NP membership follows by checking a proposed ordering; the proposal combines this with hardness to state NP-completeness. It does not transfer hardness to every small workflow model.
- Dilworth (1950): [original paper scan](https://www.math.ucsd.edu/~asuk/dilworth.pdf), first article page, states decomposition into k disjoint chains where k is the largest independent subset. Front-page metadata gives Annals of Mathematics 51(1), pp. 161–166. The ideal-prefix product and configuration bound are derived in the proposal from that chain partition; they are not presented as a separately cited runtime theorem or new contribution.
- X-Trace (2007): [original USENIX paper](https://www.usenix.org/legacy/events/nsdi07/tech/full_papers/fonseca/fonseca_html/index.html) verifies causal task-tree reconstruction and pp. 271–284. The paper header includes Ion Stoica as fifth author, whereas the newer landing page's generated BibTeX omits him. The bibliography follows the original paper.
- Dapper (2010): [Google technical report](https://research.google.com/archive/papers/dapper-2010-1.pdf) verifies authors, report identifier, scale/instrumentation/sampling scope and year. Indexed crawl dates are not publication dates.
